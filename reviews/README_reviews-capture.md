original review artifacts, not distilled. Filed to close the provenance gap

lists; these are the source reviews behind them).

  (subroutine, seeding, boundary manager), the D-46 read-only review.

  (five parts; opens "the design is not yet safe enough...").

  structural items, focused on the self-certifying evaluation problem
