# CONSUMER_ROBOTICS_MARKET
Status: BUILT
Reconciled-Against: HIP WhitePaper smart-home/robotics vertical TAM section, 2026-07-06

Clean TAM fact base for consumer and home robotics. Hard market is vacuums, mowers, security
devices, and smart-home coordination today. Humanoid/home-generalist layer is funded but still
forecast-dependent. Platform attach modelled, not reported.

---

## Bottom Line

The hard market is not humanoid maids. It is robot vacuums, mops, lawn robots, pool robots,
cameras/security robots, and smart-home systems. The platform thesis is real but still ahead of
reported market data. Do not claim a reported "household robot AI substrate TAM." Model it as an
attach layer on top of consumer robotics plus smart home.

---

## 1. Consumer And Home Robotics Market

**Finding 1: Overall consumer robotics units**

**Primary source:** ABI Research, "Consumer Robotics Shipments by Product Type: 2025 to 2030," 4Q 2025.

**Number:** ABI forecasts consumer robotics shipments growing from **68 million in 2025 to 134 million in 2030**, an **11% CAGR**. Segments include home care/lawn care, robotics toys/entertainment, and personal/social robots.

**Confirming source:** IFR, "Service Robots See Global Growth Boom," Oct. 7, 2025, reports **close to 20 million consumer service robots sold in 2024**, up **11%**, with domestic task robots the largest group.

**Caveat:** ABI is shipments, not revenue. IFR explicitly says its service robot data is sample-based, not projected to the full industry.

---

**Finding 2: Consumer robotics revenue**

**Primary source:** Grand View Research, "Consumer Robotics Market Size, Share & Trends," 2025 to 2030.

**Number:** Global consumer robotics market estimated at **$10.92B in 2024**, forecast to reach **$40.15B by 2030**, **25.0% CAGR**.

**Confirming source:** Grand View Research, "Personal and Household Service Robots Market," estimates **$18.1B in 2025** to **$90.9B by 2033**, **22.4% CAGR**.

**Caveat:** Grand View is not in the preferred primary list, and definitions vary. Use as analyst support, not the sole anchor.

---

## 2. Major Segments

**Finding 3: Vacuum and mop robots are the hard core of the market**

**Primary source:** IDC, "Worldwide Smart Home Device Market Quarterly Tracker, Q4 2024," reported via Yicai, Mar. 17, 2025.

**Number:** Global smart sweeping robot shipments reached **20.603M units in 2024**, up **11.2% YoY**. Sales reached **$9.31B**, up **19.7% YoY**, with average price **$452**.

**Confirming source:** iRobot FY2024 results show **$681.8M revenue** and roughly **2.46M robot units shipped** in 2024.

**Caveat:** IDC full report is paywalled and quoted through press/industry coverage. Roborock/Chinese brands are rapidly changing share.

---

**Finding 4: Lawn and outdoor robotics**

**Primary source:** Grand View Research, "Robotic Lawn Mowers Market," 2026 to 2033.

**Number:** Robotic lawn mowers market estimated at **$9.3B in 2025**, **$10.3B in 2026**, forecast **$22.0B by 2033**, **11.4% CAGR**.

**Confirming source:** Grand View's robotic outdoor power equipment data gives a narrower figure of **$3.69B in 2024** to **$4.97B by 2030**, **5.2% CAGR**.

**Caveat:** Definitions are inconsistent. Some include commercial landscaping, some residential only.

---

**Finding 5: Security and monitoring robots**

**Primary source:** Grand View Research, "Security Robots Market," May 2024, updated 2026.

**Number:** Global security robots market valued at **$16.5B in 2023**, forecast **$44.3B by 2030**, **14.9% CAGR**.

**Confirming source:** Reuters reported Amazon discontinued Astro for Business in 2024 to focus on household robots. Astro for Business was priced at **$2,349.99**.

**Caveat:** Security robots are mostly defense, commercial, and enterprise today. Residential security robots remain early and thin.

## 3. Humanoid And General-Purpose Home Robots

**Finding 6: Humanoid market forecast**

**Primary source:** Goldman Sachs, "The global market for humanoid robots could reach $38 billion by 2035," Feb. 27, 2024.

**Number:** Goldman forecasts **$38B humanoid robot TAM by 2035** and **1.4M unit shipments** by 2035. It expects 2030 shipments above **250,000**, mostly industrial.

**Confirming source:** Morgan Stanley, "Humanoids: A $5 Trillion Market," May 14, 2025, forecasts **more than 1B humanoids by 2050**, but only **80M in homes**.

**Caveat:** Household humanoids are the speculative part. Industrial/commercial adoption comes first.

---

**Finding 7: Household humanoid price points**

**Primary source:** 1X Technologies, "Order NEO," 2025/2026.

**Number:** 1X NEO is offered at **$20,000 ownership** or **$499/month subscription**, with **US deliveries starting 2026**.

**Confirming source:** Morgan Stanley estimates humanoid cost around **$200,000 in 2024**, falling to **$150,000 by 2028** and **$50,000 by 2050** in high-income countries.

**Caveat:** NEO is early access and uses "Expert Mode," meaning human remote supervision for complex tasks. This is not autonomous mass deployment.

---

**Finding 8: Named efforts and funding**

**Primary source:** Apptronik, "Apptronik Raises $350 Million in Series A Funding," Feb. 13, 2025.

**Number:** Apptronik raised **$350M** to scale Apollo humanoid deployment. Figure raised **$675M at a $2.6B valuation** in 2024. 1X raised **$100M Series B** to bring NEO to market.

**Confirming source:** ABI forecasts humanoid installed base rising from **24,000 in 2025 to 248,000 by 2030**, **45.5% CAGR**.

**Caveat:** These are mostly factory, logistics, guarding, and commercial pilots. Home use is still demo, preorder, and high-end beta.

**Caveat:** Broad robotics, not consumer-only. But the capital is clearly flowing into embodied AI, humanoids, and robot foundation models.

---

**Finding 10: Robot brain and platform funding**

**Primary source:** Skild AI, "Announcing our $300M Series A Funding," July 9, 2024.

**Number:** Skild raised **$300M at a $1.5B valuation** to build a general-purpose robotics foundation model.

**Confirming source:** Reuters reported Physical Intelligence raised **$400M at a $2B valuation** to build universal robot software.

**Caveat:** This validates a platform thesis for robot intelligence, but not specifically household identity, memory, and permissions yet.

## 5. Prosumer Robotics

**Finding 11: Prosumer price band exists, but market category is not clean**

**Primary source:** Unitree, "Go2 Robot Dog," current product page.

**Number:** Unitree Go2 starts at **$1,600**. Unitree G1 humanoid launched at 99,000 yuan, roughly **$16,000**. 1X NEO is **$20,000** or **$499/month**.

**Confirming source:** Grand View's personal-use healthcare companion robot segment was **$402.5M in 2024**, forecast **$987.4M by 2030**, **16.3% CAGR**.

**Caveat:** "Prosumer home robotics" is not a standardized reported category. Treat it as high-ASP early adopter demand across companion robots, robot dogs, low-cost humanoids, advanced vacuums, and hobbyist robotics.

## 6. Software And Platform Layer

**Finding 12: Robot operating layers are forming**

**Primary source:** NVIDIA, "Project GR00T Foundation Model and Isaac Robotics Platform Update," Mar. 18, 2024.

**Number:** NVIDIA frames Isaac/GR00T as a full-stack robotics platform with foundation models, simulation, Jetson Thor, and training infrastructure. GR00T N1 (2025) is open humanoid foundation model; adopters include Agility, Boston Dynamics, NEURA, XPENG.

**Confirming source:** NVIDIA Investor Relations, GR00T N1 press release, 2025.

**Caveat:** This is robot intelligence infrastructure, not household memory/permissions. The gap is above robot OS and below consumer applications.

---

**Finding 13: Smart home platforms already behave like proto-substrates**

**Primary source:** Home Assistant, "2 million homes strong," Apr. 16, 2025.

**Number:** Home Assistant reports **over 2M active installations**; survey respondents commonly had **11 to 100 devices** in households averaging **2.8 people**.

**Confirming source:** SmartThings serves **425M users worldwide** and reports **over 1B Galaxy devices and Samsung appliances connected**.

**Caveat:** These platforms coordinate devices, not full household epistemic memory, consent, private identity, and robot task context.

---

## 7. Smart Home Substrate And Integration Gap

**Finding 14: Installed smart-home base is already large**

**Primary source:** Parks Associates, CES 2024 release.

**Number:** Average US internet household had **17 connected devices in Q3 2023**.

**Confirming source:** Amazon reported customers had connected **more than 400M smart home devices to Alexa** by 2023.

**Caveat:** "Connected devices per household" includes phones, TVs, tablets, speakers, cameras, appliances, and wearables, not just IoT devices.

---

**Finding 15: Smart-home revenue base**

**Primary source:** Grand View Research, "U.S. Smart Home Market," 2025 to 2030.

**Number:** US smart home market estimated at **$23.72B in 2024**, forecast **$84.20B by 2030**, **23.4% CAGR**.

**Confirming source:** Global smart home market forecast to reach **$537.27B by 2030**, **27.0% CAGR**.

**Caveat:** Smart home market includes appliances, HVAC, security, entertainment, and services. It is substrate context, not robotics TAM.

---

**Finding 16: The integration gap is documented**

**Primary source:** IEEE/Arxiv, "Matter: IoT Interoperability for Smart Homes," 2024.

**Number:** The paper identifies "lack of interoperability" across smart home communication solutions as a persistent adoption and integration problem.

**Confirming source:** IEEE Access, "SAAC: Secure Access Control Management Framework for Multi-User Smart Home Systems," Jan. 2024, says single-user access control cannot handle conflicting demands in multi-user homes.

**Caveat:** The literature supports interoperability, multi-user permission, and privacy gaps. It does not yet name HIP's full "household AI substrate" category.

## Hard vs. Soft Assessment

**Hard / primary-sourced:** ABI/IFR unit shipments, IDC vacuum market, iRobot financials, Goldman humanoid forecast, 1X/Apptronik/Figure/Skild funding rounds, Parks Associates device density, Home Assistant install count.

**Moderately hard:** Grand View revenue forecasts for consumer robotics, lawn mowers, security robots, smart home. Good analyst sources, definitions vary, methodologies usually paywalled.

**Soft / estimated:** Platform-layer attach on household robotics. No reported category for "household AI substrate." Must model from first principles.

## Most Defensible Investor-Doc TAM Figure

Use **$40B global consumer robotics by 2030** (Grand View) as the hardware spend anchor and **134M annual consumer robot shipments by 2030** (ABI) as the unit anchor. Then state:

> "Consumer robotics is forecast to reach roughly **$40B by 2030** and **134M annual shipments**, while US smart homes are forecast to reach **$84B by 2030**. A household AI substrate is not yet a reported market category, but a **5% to 10% software/platform attach rate** on 2030 consumer robotics implies a **$2B to $4B annual platform opportunity**, before broader smart-home and care-device adjacency."

Tell it straight: real today is vacuums, mowers, security devices, companion niches, and smart-home coordination. The humanoid/home-generalist layer is exciting, funded, and still very forecast-dependent.
