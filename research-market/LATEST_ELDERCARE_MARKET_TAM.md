## 1. US Eldercare Market Size

**Primary source:** Grand View Research, "U.S. Long Term Care Market To Reach $937.56Bn By 2033," Feb. 2026.

**Number:** GVR forecasts the U.S. long-term care market to reach **$937.56B by 2033**, at **8.2% CAGR from 2026 to 2033**. Implied 2026 market size roughly **$540B**; 5-year forward estimate around **$800B by 2031**.

**Confirming source:** CMS National Health Expenditure data shows 2024 spending of **$169.4B for home health care** and **$219.9B for nursing care facilities and CCRCs**, for **$389.3B** in just those two narrower categories. CMS, "National Health Expenditures 2024 Highlights," Jan. 2026.

**Caveat:** GVR's LTC market is broader than CMS categories and includes home health, hospice, nursing care, assisted living, and other services. CMS is harder data, but narrower and excludes much private-pay assisted living/memory care.

**Segment breakout:**

| Segment | Current | Forecast | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home healthcare | $162.35B in 2024 | $381.40B by 2033, 10.0% CAGR | GVR U.S. Home Healthcare Market |
| Assisted living | $48.0B in 2025, $52.0B in 2026 | $93.5B by 2033, 8.7% CAGR | GVR U.S. Assisted Living Facility Market |
| Skilled nursing | $199.72B in 2024 | $290.02B by 2033, 4.39% CAGR | GVR U.S. Skilled Nursing Facilities Market |
| Memory care services | $6.09B in 2023, ~$7.1B in 2026 | $8.61B by 2030, 5.1% CAGR | GVR U.S. Memory Care Market |

## 2. Memory Care and Dementia

**Primary source:** Alzheimer's Association, "2026 Alzheimer's Disease Facts and Figures," 2026.

**Number:** An estimated **7.4M Americans age 65+** are living with Alzheimer's dementia in 2026; **74% are age 75+**. Health and long-term care costs for Alzheimer's and other dementias projected at **$409B in 2026** and **nearly $1T by 2050**.

**Confirming source:** CDC estimates **6.7M older adults** had Alzheimer's in the U.S.; nearly **14M** projected by 2060. CDC, "About Dementia," Aug. 2024.

**Caveat:** "Memory care market" revenue is much smaller than total dementia cost because most dementia care is delivered informally at home, through general long-term care, nursing homes, hospitals, Medicare/Medicaid, and unpaid family labor.

## 3. Demographic Curve

**Primary source:** U.S. Census Bureau, "2023 Population Projections for the Nation by Age, Sex, Race, Hispanic Origin and Nativity," Nov. 2023.

**Number:** Current ACS 2024 data shows **61.25M age 65+**, **25.66M age 75+**, and **6.34M age 85+**. By 2040, about **80.8M age 65+** and **14.4M age 85+**.

**Confirming source:** ACL's Profile of Older Americans, updated May 2024. Reports same aging curve including the 2040 expansion.

**Cohort projection table:**

| 85+ | 6.3M | ~9M | ~11M | 14.4M |

**Caveat:** 2035 breakouts require using Census projection tables directly.

## 4. Aging In Place / Home-Based Care

**Primary source:** CMS Medicaid, "Medicaid Long-Term Services and Supports Users and Expenditures by Service Category, 2023," Oct. 2025.

**Number:** In 2023, Medicaid had **8.4M HCBS users** and **$145.9B** in home- and community-based services spending, versus **1.5M institutional LTSS users** and **$82.7B** in institutional spending. HCBS represented roughly **64% of Medicaid LTSS spending**.

**Confirming source:** BLS projects home health and personal care aide employment growth of **17% from 2024 to 2034**, driven by shift toward home- and community-based settings. BLS, "Home Health and Personal Care Aides," Aug. 2025.

**Caveat:** Medicaid HCBS is not the whole private-pay eldercare market, but it is the clearest hard evidence that the system is rebalancing toward home and community care.

## 5. Family Caregiver Economics

**Primary source:** AARP Public Policy Institute, "The Economic Value of Family Caregiving," Mar. 2026.

**Number:** AARP estimates **59M family caregivers of adults** provided **49.5B hours** of care in 2024, worth **$1.01T annually** at **$20.41/hour**.

**Confirming source:** AARP/National Alliance for Caregiving reports **63M Americans** provided ongoing care in 2025, including **59M caring for someone over 18**. "Caregiving in the U.S. 2025," July 2025.

**Caveat:** This is economic value, not monetized market revenue. For TAM, it proves pain and latent demand, not immediate software spend.

---

## 6. Technology-Enabled Eldercare / Agetech

**Primary source:** Grand View Research, "U.S. Remote Patient Monitoring Software and Services Market Size & Outlook," Oct. 2024.

**Number:** U.S. RPM software and services generated **$3.76B in 2023** and are forecast to reach **$28.58B by 2030**, a **33.6% CAGR**. U.S. RPM devices add **$1.79B (2023)** to **$5.84B (2030)**, making combined RPM software/services/devices about **$5.55B current** and **$34.42B by 2030**.

**Confirming source:** AARP reports adults 50+ spent **$94.5B on tech in 2025**, expected to reach **$120B by 2030**; **55% of caregivers** use at least one technology (motion sensors, remote monitoring) to coordinate care. AARP, "Research Insights on Technology," 2026.

**Caveat:** RPM is healthcare tech, not eldercare-only. AARP tech spend is older-adult tech broadly, not care-specific. A household AI eldercare platform sits between these categories — any TAM claim needs careful wording.

## 7. Willingness To Pay / Price Points

**Primary source:** CareScout/Genworth, "Cost of Care Survey," 2025.

**Number:** Families already pay large monthly amounts:
- **$6,200/month** median assisted living
- **$35/hour** nonmedical in-home caregiver
- **$9,581/month** semi-private nursing home
- **$10,798/month** private nursing home

**Confirming source:** Medical alert subscriptions (lower-end monitoring WTP): Medical Guardian **$37.95–$39.95/month**; LifeFone home plans from **$24.95/month**, mobile/home plans ~**$47.95/month**, fall detection **$5/month**.

**Caveat:** No strong public primary source for "households will pay $X/month for AI eldercare coordination." Defensible evidence is adjacent: families pay thousands/month for care, tens/month for monitoring, and AARP shows caregivers want navigation support.

## Hard vs. Soft Assessment

**Hard / primary-sourced:** Census demographics, CMS spending, Medicaid HCBS shift, BLS employment growth, AARP caregiver count/value, CareScout care costs.

**Moderately hard:** Grand View market forecasts for home healthcare, assisted living, skilled nursing, RPM, and memory care. Good analyst sources, but definitions vary and full methodology is usually paywalled.

**Soft / estimated:** Agetech market size as a direct match for "household AI eldercare." No clean category exists yet. Must triangulate from home healthcare, RPM software/services, medical alert subscriptions, caregiver tech adoption, and dementia care navigation demand.

---

## Most Defensible Investor-Doc TAM Figure

Use **$30B by 2030** as the narrow U.S. tech-enabled home eldercare TAM, anchored to **U.S. RPM software/services forecast of $28.6B by 2030**. Then state expansion context: this sits inside a **$162B 2024 U.S. home healthcare market**, growing toward **$381B by 2033**, and a broader **$540B+ U.S. long-term care market**.
