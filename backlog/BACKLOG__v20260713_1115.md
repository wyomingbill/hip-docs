### TIER-1 Design Summary

Third axis alongside validity (built) and trust (built). Heat tracks access recency only; moves independently of the other two axes.

**The invariant (non-negotiable):** Storage heat optimizes cost and latency ONLY. It NEVER influences a governance decision. A cold fact still clears all disclosure gates if valid, in-scope, relevant, and trusted. Cold means slower to fetch (promote-on-access), never withheld. "We did not surface your shellfish allergy because it was cold" is the catastrophic failure this invariant prevents.

**Pre-build spec requirements:**
1. Access tracking per fact without hot-path cost
2. Promotion/demotion policy (promote-on-access immediate; decay time-based)
3. Tier boundaries: hot = in-memory/fast; warm = primary graph; cold = cheap deep store
4. Disclosure-blindness conformance test: cold valid fact admitted identically to hot (latency only differs)
5. Cold-fetch latency budget

**Build sequencing:** DESIGN now; BUILD after dashboard-clarity demo lands.
