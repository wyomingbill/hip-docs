- **FLAG-5** Do not use text_demo.py fields as schema authority during D-1 build. The D-1 record schema is defined in D1_RECORD_SPEC (once written); text_demo.py is the reference consumer to match, not the spec.
- **FLAG-6** Do not merge park and confirm into a single delta event. P8 park and P10 confirm/decline are separate record fields.
- **FLAG-7** Do not wire the record writer into the SIO pass. The writer fires after generation, using the InjectionResult from assemble_governed_context, not from SIO classification.
